# 🛒 DMart Sales Dashboard – Power BI

## Project Overview
The DMart Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, profit, customer types, payment methods, and product categories. It helps identify top-performing products and understand monthly sales trends.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze total net sales, orders, profit, and profit margin.
- Understand customer types and payment preferences.
- Compare sales across product categories.
- Identify the Top 10 products by profit.
- Track monthly sales trends.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Net Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin

## Dashboard Visualizations
- **Payment Modes:** Breakdown of UPI, Cash, Debit Card, and Credit Card.
- **Customer Types:** Comparison of Regular, New, and Member customers.
- **Category Sales:** Sales performance across different product categories.
- **Top 10 Products by Profit:** Highlights the most profitable products.
- **Monthly Sales Trend:** Displays sales trends from July to September 2026.

## Interactive Filters
- Date
- Category
- Payment Mode
- Customer Type

## Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## Key Insights
- Total net sales reached ₹43.5K across 94 orders.
- The overall profit margin is 5%.
- Grocery is the leading sales category in the dashboard.
- Milton Water Bottle 1 L is the highest-profit product shown.
- Regular customers form the largest customer group.
- Monthly sales show an increasing trend from July to September 2026.
